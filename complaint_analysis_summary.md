DEFINITIONS
Account Access: complaints involving a customer failing to access an account
- This category most closely aligns with Customer Service from our database
Card or Transaction Dispute:
- This category most closely aligns with Card Issue or Transaction Error from our database
Digital Banking: these complaints involve online banking platforms or in-app platforms
- This category most closely aligns with Online Banking from our database
Fees: these complaints involve fees
- This category most closely aligns with Fee Dispute from our database
Mortgage Servicing: complaints involving the servicing of a mortgage
- This category does not align with anything from our database
Service Quality: these complaints involve service provided to customers
- This category most closely aligns with Service Delay from our database

Closed: This is a case that has been resolved and closed.
Open: This is a case that has been filed and is still open.
Escalated: This is a case that has been escalated to a supervisor.
In Review: This is a case that is currently being reviewed.



OBSERVATIONS
- Mortgage servicing is the largest category at 25.83% of total cases.
- There are more cases closed than in review, escalated, or opened.
- Mortgage Servicing also has the most closed cases.
- Closed cases are fairly evently split among all categories.
