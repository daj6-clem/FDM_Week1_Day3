COMPLAINT VOLUME BY STATUS:

This is the number of complaints of each status, Open, In progress, Under review, or closed



COMPLAINT VOLUME BY CATEGORY:

This is the number of complaints of each category, Fee dispute, service delay, transaction error, online banking, card issue, customer service, fraud claim


COMPLAINT VOLUME BY MONTH:

This is the number of complaints each month. For the given dataset, this is only January.


VOLUME OF OPEN AND ESCALATED BACKLOG:

This is the number of complaints that have been opened and escalated. This does not include cases that are closed or cases that are in progress, as closed cases are not open, and in progress cases haven't been escalated.



VOLUME OF DISTINCT AFFECTED CUSTOMERS:

This is the number of distinct customers that have filed a complaint. If a customer has filed two complaints, they will only appear once.



AVERAGE CLOSED-CASE RESOLUTION TIME IN DAYS BY CATEGORY:

This is the average number of days that it took to close a given category of case. The average was calculated with julianday. This is only the number of days between the complaint's received\_at date and the corresponding closed complaint's changed\_at date. This does not include complaints that have been opened and investigated or opened and in progress.
