For five hybrid cases, sequence structured query, document retrieval and synthesis.

• State evidence returned by each stage.

• Place human approval where needed.





1. Show customer overdraft fee history and explain whether fees were assessed according to policy.

STRUCTURED QUERY

   * Customer account details
   * Overdraft occurrences
   * Fees charged
   * Fee date/amount
   * Account balance at time of fee

DOCUMENT RETRIEVAL

   * Overdraft fee policy
   * Grace period rules
   * Fee waiver eligibility criteria
   * Customer notification requirements

SYNTHESIS

   * Match fees to policy conditions
   * Identify compliant and non-compliant fees
   * Generate investigation summary
   * Recommend refund if warranted -> Human Approval
   * Escalate to Supervisor if necessary -> Human Approval
